About the publication

Marketing that survives contact with the numbers.

Marketing Insider is an independent publication for people who have to defend their work in a room with a CFO. We publish what we can evidence, we say when we can’t, and we never sell the answer.

Why we exist

Most marketing writing is either a vendor whitepaper with the logo filed off, or a thread about a tactic that worked once, for someone else, on a different product. Neither helps you decide what to do on Monday.

We started Marketing Insider in 2023 with a narrower brief: publish the work. Campaign teardowns with real numbers. Measurement models with their assumptions written down. Brand arguments that survive contact with a media plan. Every piece is written by someone who has owned the number they’re writing about.

We’re deliberately small — four desks, one essay a week, one brief every Thursday. That constraint is the product.

Editorial standards

Three rules we don’t bend

They make the writing slower and the archive more useful. Both are the point.

01

Practitioner first

Every byline belongs to someone who has run the function they’re writing about. If we can’t find that person, we don’t publish the piece.

02

Receipts over rhetoric

Numbers come with their source, sample size and the caveat that makes them less exciting. Anonymous “studies show” claims don’t make it past the first edit.

03

No pay-for-play

We take no sponsored posts, no affiliate placements and no payment for coverage. Reader subscriptions and nothing else.

Pitch us something

We commission two to four pieces a month from outside writers. What we want: a specific problem, a real dataset or campaign, and an argument that could survive being wrong.

Send a two-paragraph pitch to editors@marketinginsider.example. We reply to everything within a week. For corrections, use the same address and put “correction” in the subject — we publish fixes with the story, not quietly.
